How WhatsApp Alerts Improve School Fee Collections

One of the biggest struggles school administrations face is following up with parents for quarterly or monthly tuition fees. Traditional printed notes in diaries often go unseen, and emails are rarely opened. By integrating the WhatsApp API directly into our school fee management software, we enable you to send customized fee invoices and automated warnings to parent phone screens. This immediate communication channel has been shown to increase on-time fee recovery by up to 35% in private schools.
